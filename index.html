Blast Bunch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Blast Bunch is an offline game for Android, made by Farooq Harbeed. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: no ads, no accounts and no data collected. The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

What the game collects

Nothing. Blast Bunch has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. It does not send or receive any data over the internet, and it works without a connection.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (volumes, vibration, controls) and the last match setup you chose, so they are still there next time. This is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include these settings in your backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your account and settings; the developer never receives it.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for in-game feedback (you can turn it off in Settings) and keeps the screen on during a match. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first, with a new date above.
