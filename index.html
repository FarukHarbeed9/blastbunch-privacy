Blast Bunch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Blast Bunch is a game for Android and iPhone/iPad, made by Farooq Harbeed. This policy explains what happens with your information. The short version: the developer collects no personal data and there are no accounts. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve them, as described below.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Blast Bunch has no accounts or sign-in, no analytics of its own, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. The developer does not run any server and never receives information about you. The game itself plays fully offline.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows full-screen ads at breaks between matches and levels, never during play. It also offers optional video ads: only when you tap the button to watch one, a video plays and gives you extra energy (the in-game resource for starting matches). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). When the game has an internet connection, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares the following with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your device's advertising ID: on Android the advertising ID and app set ID (you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads); on iPhone and iPad the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (see below), and otherwise only an identifier limited to the developer's apps (IDFV);
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app data such as device type, operating system version and app version;
  • interactions with ads (app launches, ad views, taps, video views) and diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the ad SDK.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google may use it to show you personalised or non-personalised ads, depending on your choices. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Tracking on iPhone and iPad (App Tracking Transparency)

On iPhone and iPad the game first shows a short Google message explaining why it asks, then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, which asks whether Blast Bunch may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you tap Allow, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads and to measure them. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared, ads still appear and are not personalised based on tracking, and the game works exactly the same. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form when it starts, before any ad is requested. There you can agree to personalised ads, choose only non-personalised (limited) ads, or manage individual purposes and partners. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY CHOICES button in the game's Settings.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (volumes, vibration, controls), the last match setup you chose, your progress (Challenge stars and achievements) and your energy with the time it was last refilled, so they are still there next time. This is stored only in the app's own storage on your device (on iPhone and iPad, the app's preferences). Google's consent form also stores your consent choice on the device. You can remove all of it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

If you have your device's own backup turned on (Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad), this saved data may be included in that backup. It is handled by Google or Apple under your account and settings; the developer never receives it.

Permissions

The game uses the internet only for ads, the advertising ID for ads (on Android a permission; on iPhone and iPad only if you allow tracking), vibration or haptics for in-game feedback (you can turn it off in Settings), and keeps the screen on during a match. It does not access your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer collects no personal information from anyone. Ads in the game are limited to content rated up to PG (parental guidance).

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first, with a new date above.
